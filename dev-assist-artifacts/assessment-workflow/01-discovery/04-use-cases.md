### UC-16 — Draw a powered representative sample that meets the committed-lattice tiers · SAMPLE
- **Persona:** P-mlnlp-researcher (primary), P-acad-deid
- **Situation:** the tiny real corpora (i2b2-2014 ≈ 1,304 notes, TAB ≈ 1,268) cannot power per-language / per-entity / per-domain claims; the researcher needs a *representative* slice of PII-Anon v2.0.0 (575,604 records) sized so each committed cell clears its NIST power tier — without scoring the whole corpus.
- **Intent:** request the **powered-representative** preset (the **default**) and have eval-data draw a seeded, stratified sample that targets the 730 committed lattice cells at their tier targets, where each target_n is **derived** (not hand-typed) from a declared **power design point** via `required_n(p_ref, half_width, α=0.95)` per `stats/power.py::TIER_SPECS` — CRITICAL `(p_ref=0.99, half_width=0.005) → 1,522`, STANDARD `(0.98, 0.010) → 753`, LONG_TAIL `(0.95, 0.03025) → 200` positives. The design point (p_ref, half-width, α, and that tiers target a **proportion-CI half-width**, not a paired detectable effect) is written into the sample manifest. The manifest also states the **entity-type coverage envelope** of the lattice (which of the 63 types × 60 languages × 5 domains are committed), so uncovered critical types (e.g., MRN, IBAN) are not implicitly claimed as powered.
- **Outcome:** a deterministic sample manifest where every committed cell is either **WELL_POWERED** (realized positives ≥ tier target) or, when under-tier, classified as **`UNDER_SAMPLED`** (the full corpus has enough positives — fixable by drawing more) vs **`CORPUS_LIMITED`** (the full corpus itself has fewer positives than target_n — irreducible) vs **`EMPTY`** (n == 0, structural), each with its named shortfall — never silently shipped under-tier. Critical entity types outside the coverage envelope surface as **`NOT_ASSESSED`**, never silently absent.
- **Priority:** **MUST** (this is the capability's reason to exist — `workflow-maps.md` theme “Powered sampling”).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** re-running the sampler reproduces the manifest under **canonical-form equality** (sorted keys + fixed float repr) given the **same seed AND the same recorded environment/RNG fingerprint** (RNG algorithm + seed-derivation, not just the seed integer) **AND the same sampler version** (commit/hash of `subsets/slices.py` + `stats/power.py` pinned in the manifest); AND every committed cell carries a `power_class ∈ {WELL_POWERED, UNDER_SAMPLED, CORPUS_LIMITED, EMPTY}` whose `target_n` is reproduced from its design-point triple (p_ref, half_width, α), evaluated against the cell's **realized positive count** (not merely a `target_n` label), with a named shortfall on every under-tier cell and the design point + coverage envelope present in the manifest (no cell unclassified or silently under-tier; no uncovered critical type silently absent). → `True`/`False`.
- **Trace:** PGO-researcher-03, PGO-acaddeid-01 · AX-pii-anon-002, AX-pii-anon-003 · reuses `stats/power.py` (`required_n`, `TIER_SPECS`, `PowerClass`), `stats/lattice.py`, `subsets/slices.py`. · Resolves SME findings STATS-01 (design point), STATS-02 (realized positives), MEI-03 (CORPUS_LIMITED vs UNDER_SAMPLED), REPRO-03/GAME-04 (canonical-form + env/RNG + sampler-version), DOM-03 (entity-type coverage envelope / NOT_ASSESSED).

### UC-17 — Run the Elo/Glicko assessment with per-metric CIs, paired tests, and RD-convergence · RUN / SCORE / RATE
- **Persona:** P-mlnlp-researcher (primary), P-acad-deid, P-tool-builder
- **Situation:** the status quo is a hand-rolled harness, re-implemented span-F1, one or two seeds, and bespoke (or skipped) significance — numbers reviewers reject as under-powered and non-reproducible.
- **Intent:** run ≥2 systems through the existing `PIIRateEloEngine` tournament over the UC-16 powered sample; **declare the `scoring_family ∈ {anonymization, pseudonymization}`** (AX-004) so pseudonymization is scored by consistency / reversibility / linkage metrics and **never** by bare span-F1, and the two families are never merged; score spans via `compute_span_metrics` at a **declared span-match mode** (exact vs relaxed/overlap); attach a CI to every metric; report Glicko **RD-convergence** with a bounded stopping rule; and back every system-vs-system claim with a paired test under a **declared multiplicity family**.
- **Outcome:** a results artifact in which (a) each reported metric carries a CI under a **deterministic interval-selection rule** — proportions → **Wilson** (default), small-n / boundary → **Clopper-Pearson**, paired differences → **paired-bootstrap** — with **two explicit inference families declared**: per-cell CIs are **simultaneous-uncorrected / exploratory** (labelled as such, not headline claims), while confirmatory **pairwise** claims carry a **Holm–Bonferroni** verdict with the **family size reported** (# pairs × metrics in the confirmatory set); (b) each pairwise claim carries a McNemar + paired-bootstrap verdict; (c) the tournament's RD-convergence status is reported (converged vs **NOT-converged**, honestly) under a stated stopping rule, and a NOT-converged verdict propagates as a **blocking honesty flag into UC-23**; (d) each system carries a **`contamination_status` ∈ {disclosed-unseen, disclosed-trained-on-corpus, undisclosed}** (the public synthetic corpus invites train-on-test inflation), with non-`disclosed-unseen` ratings flagged contamination-uncontrolled downstream; and (e) the CI is scoped **conditional-on-this-sample**, with corpus-draw variance addressed by **either** ≥2 sampling seeds + **rank-stability (Kendall-τ)** reported **or** an explicit `seed_variance_scope: single-seed` flag carried to UC-23 as **rank-volatility: UNMEASURED**.
- **Priority:** **MUST** (the academic-soundness spine of the 3 HIGH personas).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** for the run, the **`scoring_family`** is declared and pseudo runs are NOT scored by bare span-F1; the **span-match mode** is declared; **every** emitted rating/metric has a non-null CI assigned by the deterministic selection rule (`method ∈ {wilson, clopper-pearson, paired-bootstrap}`) and labelled by inference family (per-cell exploratory vs confirmatory pairwise); **every** confirmatory pairwise system claim has a McNemar p-value AND a **Holm–Bonferroni** decision with the **family size** stated; the report states the `ConvergenceChecker` verdict (`is_converged` + `max_rd` vs `rd_threshold`) **and** the **stopping rule** (max rounds / seed budget), with NOT-converged linked into UC-23; **every** system carries a `contamination_status`; AND the run records `seed_variance_scope` (multi-seed Kendall-τ present, or single-seed flagged) — any missing CI, undeclared scoring family/span-mode/multiplicity-family-size, missing paired test, absent convergence verdict or stopping rule, missing contamination_status, or missing seed-variance scope makes it `False`.
- **Trace:** PGO-researcher-01/03, PGO-builder-01, PGO-acaddeid-01 · AX-pii-anon-003, AX-pii-anon-004 · reuses `engine.py`, `convergence.py`, `metrics_bridge.py`, `significance.py` (extend for Holm), `stats/intervals.py`, `stats/paired.py`. · Resolves SME findings STATS-03/MEI-01 (multiplicity families + size + Holm), GAME-01 (contamination_status), STATS-04/GAME-02 (seed-variance scope + Kendall-τ), REPRO-02 (stopping rule + non-convergence propagation), STATS-05 (interval-selection rule), DOM-01 (scoring_family / AX-004 enforced), DOM-04 (span-match mode), GAME-06 (per-slice wins carry adjusted verdict via the exploratory/confirmatory split).

### UC-18 — Pre-register the run BEFORE any system is scored · X / REPORT
- **Persona:** P-acad-deid (primary), P-mlnlp-researcher, P-complreviewer (assurance sub-archetype of P-dpo)
- **Situation:** reviewers (and an EU-AI-Act Art. 11 assessor) reject results that look chosen after the fact; the workflow needs a tamper-evident commitment to the sample, seed, systems, metrics, and stopping rule made *before* scores exist.
- **Intent:** emit a **pre-registration record** at the end of `sample` and freeze it before the first system runs. The payload pins not only the design (dataset **version** + sample-manifest hash + seed + system list + metric set + RD **stopping rule**) but the **full analysis plan** that otherwise creates researcher degrees of freedom: **interval-selection rule per metric class**, **multiplicity family + size**, **power design point**, **span-match mode**, **scoring family**, and **tie/exclusion rules**. The payload is committed and the commit **pushed to remote**, so ordering is anchored to a git SHA rather than a back-datable local clock.
- **Outcome:** a pre-registration artifact whose **commit SHA + hash** are referenced by the final report and chained into the first run-record (pre-reg → first run-record), so anyone can confirm the scored design + analysis plan equal the pre-registered design — and that the pre-reg existed before any score. Each pre-registration is **immutable**; the report discloses the **count of prior pre-registered runs for the same system set (run lineage)** so re-roll-until-favorable cherry-picking is visible.
- **Priority:** **MUST** (academic-soundness bar: “the run is pre-registered + reproducible from the manifest”).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** the pre-registration is bound to a **git commit SHA pushed to remote** that is **chained into the first run-record** (the first `run`/`score` record references the pre-reg commit; a back-datable timestamp is at most a secondary check) — i.e. ordering is commit-anchored, **tamper-evident only via that anchor**; AND the final report embeds the pre-registration hash + commit SHA and a `pre_registration_matches: true` check whose equality set includes **dataset_version + sample-manifest hash + seed + system list + metric set + rd_stopping_rule + interval-selection rule + multiplicity family/size + power design point + span-match mode + scoring family + tie/exclusion rules**; AND the report states the **run-lineage count** for the system set — any missing pinned field, an un-pushed/unchained commit, or an absent lineage count makes it `False`. → `True`/`False`.
- **Trace:** PGO-acaddeid-03, PGO-complreviewer-01 · AX-pii-anon-002 · reuses `scripts/write_manifest.py` pattern. · Resolves SME findings MEI-02/REPRO-01 (commit-anchor + hash-chain, dataset_version + rd_stopping_rule in match set), STATS-06/DOM-05 (full analysis plan in payload + match set), GAME-05 (immutable pre-reg + run-lineage disclosure). Applies to full-corpus runs too (parity — see UC-19).

### UC-19 — Full-corpus opt-in run (the “no sampling, score everything” mode) · SAMPLE / RUN
- **Persona:** P-acad-deid (primary), P-mlnlp-researcher
- **Situation:** for a definitive headline result a researcher sometimes wants the entire 575,604-record corpus scored, not a representative sample — but this must be a deliberate **opt-in**, never the default (cost/runtime), and must use the same seeded, manifest-stamped machinery.
- **Intent:** select the **full-corpus** preset explicitly and run the identical `load → … → report` pipeline over all records, with the run-record stamping `preset=full` and the full record count — and, **for parity with UC-18, pre-register the run** (a census still freezes systems / metrics / analysis plan before scoring).
- **Outcome:** a full-corpus run that is reproducible-from-manifest, **pre-registered like a sampled run**, and reportable on the same footing, with the throughput/runtime cost recorded. Its metrics are declared **exact-on-corpus (descriptive census)** by default — **no CI** — and CIs are retained **only** when the report explicitly states the inferential target is a **super-population** (future inputs), not the fixed corpus.
- **Priority:** **SHOULD** (real demand, but the powered sample is the default path; full-corpus is the opt-in).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** invoking the full-corpus preset produces a run-record with `preset == "full"` and **`records_scored == dataset.record_count(version)`** (resolved from the version-pinned loader, **not** a literal `575604`), AND the run is NOT reachable without the explicit opt-in flag (default invocation yields the representative sample), AND a **pre-registration exists** for the run (UC-18 parity), AND the report declares the inferential target (exact-on-corpus → metrics labelled descriptive/no-CI; super-population → CIs retained). → `True`/`False`.
- **Trace:** PGO-acaddeid-01/03, PGO-researcher-01 · AX-pii-anon-002 · reuses `scripts/benchmark_throughput.py` run-record. · Resolves SME findings REPRO-04 (`dataset.record_count(version)` not a magic constant + UC-18 pre-reg parity), STATS-08 (descriptive-census vs super-population inferential target).

### UC-20 — Smoke run for fast iteration (CI / local dev) · SAMPLE / RUN
- **Persona:** P-tool-builder (primary), P-mlnlp-researcher
- **Situation:** a tool maintainer iterating on a recognizer needs a fast, deterministic end-to-end pass in CI/local dev to catch breakage in seconds — the powered or full runs are too slow for an inner loop.
- **Intent:** select the **smoke** preset — a tiny seeded fixed slice that exercises the whole spine (`load → sample → run → score → rate → report`) quickly, explicitly flagged as **not statistically valid**.
- **Outcome:** a fast green/red signal that the pipeline runs end-to-end, with the artifact self-labelling “smoke — not powered, not citable.” To prevent a smoke output from ever *looking* inferential, smoke artifacts **suppress / null all CI and p-value fields** — not merely carry a caveat.
- **Priority:** **SHOULD** (developer-velocity / CI enabler; not a results path).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** the smoke preset completes the full spine on a fixed seeded slice and its output artifact carries `preset == "smoke"` AND a non-strippable `not_statistically_valid: true` (or equivalent “smoke / not powered” caveat) **AND every CI field and p-value field is suppressed/null** — so a smoke result can never be mistaken for, or surface numbers that look like, a powered result. → `True`/`False`.
- **Trace:** PGO-builder-01 · AX-pii-anon-002 · mirrors `subsets/slices.py` non-strippable-caveat pattern. · Resolves SME finding STATS-09 (suppress CI/p-value emission in smoke, not just flag).

### UC-23 — Produce a reportable leaderboard + figures with HONEST verdicts · REPORT / X
- **Persona:** P-mlnlp-researcher (primary), P-tool-builder, P-dpo, P-priv-eng
- **Situation:** a run is only useful if it ends in something a paper, model card, or DPIA can cite — a ranked leaderboard with figures — but it MUST NOT over-claim: synthetic-only, under-power, and non-convergence have to ride the headline, and a recall-vs-precision operating point must be shown (the false-positive tax), never a bare single F1.
- **Intent:** render a leaderboard + figures from the UC-17 results that surfaces per-slice strengths and an operating-point view that **names the de-id recall/precision asymmetry** (a missed SSN is a breach, a false positive is over-redaction) — a **recall-priority Fβ (β>1)** or a stated **FN:FP cost**, plus a **threshold-free AUPRC** alongside the **pre-registered operating point** (threshold rule fixed pre-scoring, UC-18) so the curve is not one point post-hoc-tuned to the eval set. Ranks are **gated by the paired-test verdict**: systems whose pairwise difference is not significant (post Holm–Bonferroni) are grouped/greyed as **statistical ties**. The report attaches the honest verdicts: **RD-NOT-CONVERGED** (propagated from UC-17, if so), **UNDER-POWERED / UNDER-SAMPLED / CORPUS-LIMITED** (per flagged cell), **contamination-uncontrolled** (per non-disclosed-unseen system), **rank-volatility: UNMEASURED** (if single-seed), **worst-language / low-resource recall** (cross-lingual collapse is masked by the aggregate otherwise), and the **non-strippable synthetic-only caveat** (`DesignProvenance`, AX-001/003).
- **Outcome:** a citable, **self-verifying** report that embeds the **pre-registration hash + run id** (UC-18 / UC-22) so it is reproducible-from-manifest end-to-end; every ranked number carries its CI, no rank out-runs the paired evidence, and the honesty flags travel with the artifact — no over-claim survives into the record.
- **Priority:** **MUST** (the report is the deliverable; honest verdicts are the academic-soundness bar and the P-dpo/P-complreviewer evidence requirement).
- **Acceptance signal (boolean):** the rendered report (a) shows each system's metric **with its CI**, a **recall-priority Fβ (β>1) or stated FN:FP cost**, AND a **threshold-free AUPRC** alongside the **pre-registered operating point** (not a lone F1, not a post-hoc threshold); (b) **gates ranks by the paired-test verdict** (non-significant pairs grouped/greyed as ties); (c) carries the non-strippable synthetic-only caveat on every emitted figure (cannot be serialized away); (d) prints the convergence verdict + any UNDER-POWERED/UNDER-SAMPLED/CORPUS-LIMITED cell flags, the `contamination_status` flags, the rank-volatility scope, and the worst-language/low-resource recall; and (e) **embeds the pre-registration hash + run id** — stripping any honesty signal (synthetic-only caveat / convergence verdict / under-power flag / contamination flag / rank-volatility scope), un-gating a rank from its paired test, omitting the AUPRC/Fβ asymmetry view, or dropping the pre-reg-hash + run-id binding makes the artifact invalid. → `True`/`False`.
- **Scope boundary (honest bound):** this report scores **detection / anonymization** power only. **Re-identification power** (the separate `stats/power.py::REID_TIER_SPECS` ladder — operating point p≈0.1–0.5, 897/385 *pairs*, distinct from the 0.95–0.99 detection tiers) is **OUT of scope for CAP-02 v0.1** and is a named roadmap item; CAP-02 does not emit re-id-power verdicts (RRS scenarios live in cycle-1 UC-08/09). Stated explicitly so the bound is not silent.
- **Trace:** PGO-researcher-01, PGO-builder-02, PGO-dpo-01/02, PGO-priveng-01 · AX-pii-anon-001/003 · reuses `scoring/detection.py::DesignProvenance`, `reporting/*`, `convergence.py`. · Resolves SME findings STATS-07 (rank gated by paired test / ties greyed), DOM-02 (recall/precision asymmetry — Fβ β>1 / FN:FP cost), GAME-03/MEI-05 (pre-registered operating point + AUPRC, not a faux curve), STATS-04/GAME-02 (rank-volatility scope surfaced), REPRO-07 (embed pre-reg hash + run id), DOM-07 (worst-language / low-resource recall), MEI-06 (re-id power explicitly out-of-scope).
